OUR APPROACH

From Questions to Practical Understanding

A useful research process should be structured, transparent, and open to learning. Our intended approach emphasizes understanding the problem before developing or recommending a solution.

The process may vary depending on the research question, available resources, technical requirements, and any ethical or legal considerations.

Discuss a Research Idea
01

Identify the Problem

Define the challenge, the people affected, and the question to investigate.

02

Explore Existing Knowledge

Review relevant documentation, research, tools, and established technical practices.

03

Develop an Approach

Identify suitable methods, requirements, constraints, and evaluation criteria.

04

Test and Evaluate

Where feasible, use appropriate tests and observations to evaluate a proposed solution.

05

Document and Share

Record methods, limitations, and findings, and share verified results when they are ready.

RESPONSIBILITY FIRST

Innovation Must Be Responsible

Technological progress should respect people, protect information, and consider the consequences of how systems are designed and used.

Privacy Protection

Respect personal information and use appropriate safeguards when handling sensitive data.

Ethical Conduct

Consider fairness, transparency, accountability, and potential consequences when exploring technological solutions.

Authorized Testing

Conduct security testing and digital investigations only within appropriate legal permissions and clearly defined boundaries.

Honest Reporting

Distinguish verified findings from assumptions, proposed concepts, and work that remains incomplete.
